Privacy

Your answers stay on your device.

This game is built so that you can answer honestly without worrying who sees it. Here is exactly what that means, including the parts that are up to you.

What the game collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, no names, no company fields and no email. The game's code has no cookies, no saved storage, no analytics and no advertising trackers. Your answers live only in the memory of the page you are looking at. Close the tab, refresh, or press Clear everything, and they are gone.

What it notices while you play

To flag low-quality answers, the page notes how fast you answer and which screen position you tap. This stays in the page's memory only. It is never saved or sent, and it disappears with the page.

What can still be seen

  • The host. Whoever hosts this site can see the usual server records: that a device requested a page, and the address requested. Your answers are never part of a page request.
  • Your employer's network. If you play on a work device or network, your employer may be able to log that you visited this site. They cannot see your answers. If that concerns you, use a personal device and network.

Your result stays on your screen

Your result is shown only on this page. Nothing is exported, stored or sent. When you close the tab or press Clear everything, it is gone.

What this is not

It is a conversation tool. It is not an HR tool, a performance tool or legal evidence, and it says nothing about any individual. Read how scoring works.
