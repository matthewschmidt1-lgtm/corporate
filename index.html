The difference

Same industry. Same quarter. Different company.

Nobody sets out to build a bureaucracy. It accumulates, like lint, and underneath it is usually a company that forgot why it was built.

Priorities

BureaucracyFifteen. All labeled critical.

EntrepreneurialTwo. Everyone can name them.

The customer

BureaucracySix segments and a forty slide deck.

EntrepreneurialOne person. Someone has met them.

Features

BureaucracyAdded by committee. Removed never.

EntrepreneurialRemoved on a Tuesday.

Decisions

BureaucracyPre-aligned, socialized, then tabled.

EntrepreneurialMade by one person, out loud.

Quality

BureaucracyFine, within tolerance.

EntrepreneurialFix the part nobody sees.

The team

BureaucracyMeasured in headcount.

EntrepreneurialMeasured in what ships.

Failure

BureaucracyRenamed a "learning" and never discussed.

EntrepreneurialStudied, then used.

The experience

BureaucracyOwned by five departments, so by none.

EntrepreneurialOwned end to end.

The final lesson

Think differently.

Don't spend your life trying to become the next Steve Jobs.
Become someone who sees what others overlook.
