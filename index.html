The lessons

Five questions in the spirit of Jobs.

The standard the game measures against. Each one plays in a few seconds and ends with a move for Monday.

  1. Focus. Fifteen priorities. Two survive, and everyone can name them.
  2. Customer. The customer is not in the meeting until someone brings them in.
  3. Simplicity. Seven layers. One carries the value. Remove the rest on purpose.
  4. Excellence. Finish the back of the cabinet. Nobody checks. That is the point.
  5. Innovation. Most limits are old habits with good posture. Walk through one.
