How scoring works

Each answer is scored by how well it fits the stakes of the situation, so a careful answer on a risky decision scores well and a reckless one does not. Every principle counts equally. The report gives a range, a confidence level, and the pattern behind it: slow structure versus toxic behavior. It is a conversation tool, not a validated psychological test. Read the full method.

What it measures

The detector asks one thing: is this company designed for the customer, or for itself? Eleven principles, drawn from the lessons of Steve Jobs's career, are tested by the cards:

    Structure is not the enemy. Financial controls, safety, compliance and governance score well here. The target is structure that creates no value. We also do not reward aggression, intimidation or impossible deadlines: standards are only high when people are treated with respect.
