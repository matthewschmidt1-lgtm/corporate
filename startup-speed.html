Startup Speed

Can a big company still think like a small one?

Eight tests, each with one move you can make this week.

  1. 01

    The 100 to 3 Test

    Your company has 100 priorities. Keep three. What survives?

    Your move. List your team's priorities. Cross out everything you would not defend to a customer. Keep three.

  2. 02

    The Customer in the Room

    When an important decision is made, is someone there who truly knows the customer's experience?

    Your move. At the next decision meeting, name who speaks for the customer. If no one can, that is your first fix.

  3. 03

    The 30 Day Experiment

    Can a small team test a promising idea in 30 days without asking twelve people?

    Your move. Pick one idea. Set a 30 day test and a stop date. Tell people the plan instead of asking for permission.

  4. 04

    The Kill Your Own Project Test

    Can leadership cancel a project that is not working, even if someone senior championed it?

    Your move. Name one project nobody would start today. Say so out loud, with the numbers.

  5. 05

    The Department-Free Test

    Can a customer's problem get solved without the customer learning your org chart?

    Your move. Trace one customer problem across every team it touched. Name one owner for the whole path.

  6. 06

    The Details Nobody Sees

    Does the company care about quality the customer may never notice?

    Your move. Fix one detail no customer will check, and tell the team why.

  7. 07

    The Sacred Cow Test

    Can someone challenge a 20 year old process without becoming an outcast?

    Your move. Ask of one old process: real limit, or old habit? Try dropping it for a month.

  8. 08

    The Ego-Free Test

    Can someone disagree with the boss, prove them wrong, and still be rewarded for making the company better?

    Your move. Next time you are wrong, say so first. Thank the person who showed you.

Never reward cruelty. Structure is not the enemy. Controls that protect money, safety and the law are part of a good company.

Now see how yours holds up.
