The talks

In his own words.

Seven talks from 1981 to 2008, read as one body of work: the ideas he kept coming back to, in his own words.

Seven talks, 27 years, and a man who never changed his mind about what mattered. The computer is a bicycle for the mind, so make it simple outside however hard it is inside. Let customers tell you what you are good at. Great costs no more than junk. Hire a few people better than the job and argue until they agree. Bet on a few things. Own your mistakes. Fear comfort, because comfort is how a company stops serving the people it was built for.

Seven talks, seven ideas so far. More as they turn up.
